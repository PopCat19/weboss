Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Click circles when the outer ring closes on them. Hold and follow sliders. Spin the spinner by rotating your cursor. Default keys are Z, X, and mouse buttons. Change keybindings in Settings.

The game is laggy. What can I do?

Disable "Use System Resolution" in Settings and lower the resolution scale. Close other browser tabs. Use a modern browser (Firefox or Chrome). Try enabling "Raw Cursor Input" to bypass OS cursor processing.

Cursor movement feels delayed?

Enable "Raw Cursor Input" in Settings to bypass system acceleration and smoothing. This may help on lower-end systems. Note: cursor size options are limited with this mode.

Nothing shows up on the page?

The beatmap mirror (catboy.best) may be temporarily down. Wait and try again later. You can still play beatmaps you've favorited — they're stored locally.

Audio and video are out of sync?

Adjust "Audio Offset" in Settings. Use bemuse sync to measure your system's keyboard delay first. Not recommended otherwise.

How are favorites stored?

Favorites and play history are saved in your browser's local storage. They won't sync across devices. Click the heart icon on any beatmap to favorite or unfavorite it.

Where do beatmaps come from?

Beatmaps are served from the Mino mirror. This site is a fork of webosu with improvements from BlaNKtext/webosu.
